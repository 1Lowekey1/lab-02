Who am I?

I'm Nathaniel Glenn P. Sevilla, a casual developer from Davao City, Philippines. I enjoy building things for the web, mostly on the backend side where the real magic happens — APIs, databases, server logic, and all the things users never see but always depend on.

When I'm not coding, I'm probably starting a new project, forgetting about it, and then rediscovering it months later wondering what past-me was thinking. It's a cycle, and I've embraced it.
